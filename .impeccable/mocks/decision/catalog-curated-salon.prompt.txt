Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: full-fidelity desktop web design concept for the separate /catalogo route of Lirios.
Input images: Image 1 is the existing Lirios homepage and is the binding visual identity reference; Images 2–4 are real chenille-flower product photography references and should preserve the handmade fuzzy pipe-cleaner material and realistic product scale.
Primary request: Design a polished 16:10 desktop catalog first viewport called “Salón curado”. This is a small collection of roughly twenty arrangements and individual flowers, composed like a refined florist lookbook rather than ecommerce inventory.
Scene/backdrop: matte blush cream #faf2eb, deep wine #6c1326, sand accents, charcoal reading ink, square edges and hairline rules; inherit the exact calm editorial character of the Lirios homepage.
Structure: compact header with Lirios wordmark, navigation, and “Carrito (0)”. Large but restrained heading “La colección” and short Spanish sentence. Under it, one minimal horizontal filter ledger with categories “Todos / Arreglos / Flores individuales”, then “Color”, “Tipo de flor”, and “Ordenar: precio”. Below, create a spacious asymmetric salon hang: a tall portrait arrangement at large scale, one wide close-up of an individual flower, one smaller arrangement, all offset vertically with substantial negative space and no shared card shells. Product captions live directly beneath or beside photography: serif name, small sans category, provisional Quetzal price such as “Q 185” or “Q 42”. Include “Precios provisionales”.
Interaction cue: one hovered image has only a fine wine underline and the words “Ver de cerca”; product click opens a full-screen photo modal but the overlay is not open in this concept.
Style/medium: shippable high-end editorial ecommerce UI, airy museum-salon composition, Bodoni-like high-contrast serif for voice, Manrope-like sans for controls, real naturally lit chenille flowers, precise typographic alignment.
Composition/framing: landscape browser viewport, straight-on, no device chrome, full page edge to edge; show approximately five products with deliberately different image dimensions, never a uniform grid.
Constraints: preserve established Lirios identity from Image 1; all corners square; readable Spanish labels; prices visible; no availability; no search; no sidebar; no cards; no equal columns; no repeated buttons; no gradients; no glass; no rounded pills; no ratings; no badges; no promotional copy; no watermark.
