Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: full-fidelity desktop web design concept for the separate /catalogo route of Lirios.
Input images: Image 1 is the existing Lirios homepage and is the binding visual identity reference; Images 2–4 are real chenille-flower product photography references and should supply the product character and visual realism, not be copied into one repeated product.
Primary request: Design a polished 16:10 desktop catalog first viewport called “Capítulos de color”. This is a small, curated collection of about twenty products, not a dense ecommerce marketplace.
Scene/backdrop: matte blush cream #faf2eb page, compact header inherited from the reference, deep wine #741429 typography and actions, powder blush and sand tonal passages, square corners, hairline rules.
Structure: restrained header with Lirios wordmark, navigation, bag icon and “Carrito (0)”. Then a generous title “La colección” with one short Spanish sentence. Below it place a single-line filter console: category segmented text “Todos / Arreglos / Flores individuales”, “Color”, “Tipo de flor”, and “Ordenar: precio”. The main catalog begins immediately as a chromatic editorial chapter titled “Tonos cálidos”: one very large product photograph occupies about half the width and two supporting product photographs sit beside it at unequal scale. Continue just enough of a second chapter at the lower fold to imply the page rhythm. No repeated card boxes.
Product captions: Bodoni-style serif names, small sans label “Arreglo” or “Flor individual”, and provisional Quetzal prices such as “Q 185” and “Q 42”. Add a discreet line “Precios provisionales”.
Interaction cue: one selected product shows a subtle hover label “Ver de cerca”; clicking would open a full-screen modal, but do not show the modal in this concept.
Style/medium: shippable editorial ecommerce UI mockup, refined florist art direction, high-contrast Didone headlines and calm humanist sans controls, precise generous spacing, luminous real product photography, natural shadows.
Composition/framing: landscape browser viewport, straight-on, no device frame, no perspective, full page edge to edge.
Constraints: preserve the established Lirios visual identity from Image 1; all corners square; only one filled wine action in the viewport; filters must be obvious and usable; readable Spanish text; show product prices but no availability; no search field; no sidebar; no product-card grid; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no rounded pills; no ratings; no discount badges; no promotional banners; no invented claims; no watermark.
