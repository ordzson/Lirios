Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: full-fidelity desktop web design concept for the separate /catalogo route of Lirios.
Input images: Image 1 is the binding Lirios homepage identity reference; Images 2–4 are product photography references for handmade chenille flowers.
Primary request: Design a polished 16:10 desktop catalog first viewport called “Índice vivo”. It must make a collection of about twenty items efficient to browse without looking like an ecommerce card grid.
Scene/backdrop: matte blush cream #faf2eb, deep wine #741429, parchment and charcoal, square corners, hairline rules, restrained photographic depth. Match the established Lirios identity exactly.
Structure: compact top header with Lirios wordmark, navigation, and “Carrito (0)”. Beneath, use a two-pane editorial selector across the viewport. Left pane about 42% wide: title “La colección”, a short Spanish sentence, category tabs “Todos / Arreglos / Flores individuales”, compact dropdown controls “Color”, “Tipo de flor”, “Ordenar: precio”, then a typographic numbered list of product names and provisional prices: “01 Lirios carmesí — Q 185”, “02 Girasol de otoño — Q 42”, “03 Lirios rosados — Q 165”, “04 Rama de ciruelo — Q 38”. One row is active with a wine underline and tiny “Arreglo” label. Right pane about 58% wide: one dominant editorial photograph of the active product, with a narrow strip of two alternate photo thumbnails, product name, provisional price, and a single wine action “Ver de cerca”. Add discreet text “Precios provisionales”. The action implies a full-screen modal with multiple images and quantity, but do not show the modal open.
Style/medium: shippable refined florist interface, magazine index meets selection table, high-contrast Bodoni-like serif for headings and product names, calm Manrope-like sans for operation, luxurious real chenille texture and natural light.
Composition/framing: landscape browser viewport, straight-on, no device frame, full page edge to edge. Make the split clear and calm with abundant whitespace. Only one product image dominates.
Constraints: preserve Lirios palette and typography from Image 1; all corners square; filters visible and understandable; prices shown, no availability; no search field; no product cards; no multi-column thumbnail grid; no ratings; no badges; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no rounded pills; no promotional banner; no invented claims; no watermark.
