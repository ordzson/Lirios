Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: full-fidelity desktop web design proposal for the separate /catalogo route of Lirios.
Input images: Image 1 is the chosen “Capítulos de color” concept to evolve; Image 2 is the binding Lirios homepage identity; Images 3–4 are real product references. Preserve the brand and chenille material.
Primary request: Create proposal “Tres escaparates”, a more traditional and immediately understandable evolution of Capítulos de color for a small catalog of about twenty products.
Structure: compact Lirios header with navigation and “Carrito (0)”. Large title “La colección” with one short Spanish sentence and discreet “Precios provisionales”. Immediately below, a clean three-part photographic category gateway with equal visual importance: “Arreglos listos”, “Flores individuales”, “Arreglos personalizados”. Each category is one large square-cornered editorial image with its title and a simple wine arrow link. “Arreglos personalizados” says “Diseña el tuyo” and routes to a future separate page; do not show custom products or prices. Beneath the gateway, show a restrained catalog toolbar: tabs “Todos / Arreglos / Flores individuales”, dropdowns “Color”, “Tipo de flor”, “Ordenar: precio”. At the lower fold begin the product collection with one large arrangement and two smaller individual flowers, captioned with name, type and provisional Quetzal price.
Visual system: matte blush cream #faf2eb, deep wine #741429, charcoal text, Bodoni-like serif headlines, Manrope-like controls, hairline rules, square corners, natural product photography and generous editorial spacing. More traditional navigation, never marketplace density.
Composition: landscape 16:10 browser viewport, straight-on, no browser chrome, edge-to-edge page. The three category images should be the clearest element after the title.
Constraints: readable Spanish, show prices only on ready products; no availability; no search; no sidebar; no rounded cards or pills; no ratings; no badges; no discounts; no gradients; no glass; no invented claims; no watermark.
